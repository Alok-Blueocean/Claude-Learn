## Anthropic documentation (public, verifiable)

The exam guide itself directs candidates to "review official Anthropic documentation and help articles
for Claude features such as Projects, Artifacts, Memory, Skills, and Code Execution" (Section 7, How to
Prepare). The following are real Anthropic documentation entry points relevant to the domains in this
kit. Only URLs the author is confident are genuine Anthropic properties are listed; deep-linked article
paths can change over time, so if a specific link has moved, use the site's own search/navigation from
its root domain rather than assuming the content is gone.

**Developer/product documentation (docs.claude.com)** — relevant to Domains 1–3 (prompting
technique, model differentiation):

- https://docs.claude.com/ — documentation home.
- https://docs.claude.com/en/docs/about-claude/models/overview — overview of the Claude model
  family (Haiku / Sonnet / Opus) and their relative trade-offs, relevant to Domain 3.
- https://docs.claude.com/en/docs/build-with-claude/prompt-engineering/overview — prompt
  engineering overview, relevant to Domain 1.
- https://docs.claude.com/en/docs/build-with-claude/prompt-engineering/be-clear-and-direct —
  guidance on writing clear, direct prompts, relevant to Domain 1.
- https://docs.claude.com/en/docs/build-with-claude/prompt-engineering/use-xml-tags — guidance
  on structuring prompts with clear instruction/data separation, relevant to Domain 1 and the
  prompt-injection awareness discussed in Domain 6/scenario_qa.
- https://docs.claude.com/en/docs/build-with-claude/prompt-engineering/chain-of-thought —
  guidance on step-by-step reasoning prompts, relevant to Domain 1 (task decomposition).
- https://docs.claude.com/en/docs/build-with-claude/prompt-engineering/multishot-prompting —
  guidance on using examples to steer output format/quality, relevant to Domain 1 and 7.

**Anthropic policy and trust resources** — relevant to Domain 6 (governance, risk, responsible use):

## Note on scope

This kit deliberately does not cite third-party blog posts, unofficial exam-dump sites, or any resource
claiming to reproduce live exam content — the Exam Guide's confidentiality terms (Section 13) prohibit
disclosure of actual exam items, and no such items are used or implied anywhere in this kit. All
practice and scenario content in `practice_questions.md` and `scenario_qa.md` is original material
written against the public blueprint, not reproductions of protected exam content.
